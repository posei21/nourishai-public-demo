Overview

What this page shows

The goal is simple: show the app clearly, show state changing on screen, and keep the public boundary obvious.

Core product surfaces

  • Daily Dash scheduling, nutrient rings, and progress feedback
  • Meal Hub planning, grocery, and prep flows
  • Fitness Hub focus, execution, and logged outcome states
  • Profile and settings controls that shape generation behavior

What is included

  • Recorded Aurora walkthrough using a seeded public-safe account
  • Curated screenshots from the same product surface area
  • Before and after state changes for nutrition actions
  • A side-by-side look at the alternate visual themes

Release boundary

  • No source code, backend logic, prompts, or internal tooling
  • No cloud configuration, endpoints, or credentials
  • No analytics exports, tester data, or operations material
  • Static HTML, CSS, video, and screenshots only
